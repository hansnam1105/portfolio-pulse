What to look at in this alpha
  • Gain/loss colour. Red = up, blue = down (Korean convention, applied to KR and US holdings alike). Every figure also carries a +/− sign, an arrow, and a hidden word (상승/하락/보합) — squint or go greyscale and the numbers still read correctly.
  • Red never means "bad" here. The validation error in 거래 입력 uses a different red (--color-danger) from the gain red (--color-value-up) on purpose.
  • Uncertainty is visible. 추정 수량 (back-derived share count) and 시세 지연 (a provider failed) are text badges with their own tokens, not greyed-out or italicised.
  • The 거래일 field is deliberately prominent and back-datable — the transaction date decides ordering and supersession, so it can't be hidden or pinned to "now".
  • No login anywhere — auth was removed from the design in spec v3.
